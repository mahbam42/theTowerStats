# Code of Conduct

The Tower Stats community exists to support a project built on trust, clarity, and respect for player autonomy.
We prioritize descriptive, evidence-based discussion over prescriptive guidance, and we treat each other with the same care we expect from the app itself.

## Our Standards

- Be professional, respectful, and inclusive.
- Keep feedback factual, specific, and focused on the work.
- Preserve traceability: cite sources and distinguish fact from inference.
- Avoid prescriptive or authoritative gameplay advice presented as “the correct way.”
- Respect player privacy and do not share private data without consent.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Misrepresenting data or presenting speculation as verified results.
- Pressure to adopt a “best build” or ranking mentality.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers at mahbam42+tts@gmail.com.
